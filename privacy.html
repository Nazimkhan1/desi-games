Privacy Policy — Desi Games

App: desi-games.nextgrade.in · Last updated: 28 November 2026

Desi Games is a free collection of traditional Indian games. You can play without any login. This page explains what data the app uses.

1. Playing without login

  • Your name, chosen face, XP, coins, badges and game records are saved only on your phone (browser storage). We do not receive them.
  • Your language and sound choice are also saved on your phone.

2. Online games with friends

  • To play online, the app uses Google Firebase with an anonymous ID (no name or email).
  • During an online game, the moves, the room code and your app name and face are sent through Firebase so your friend's phone can show them. Only people with the room code join that room.

3. Optional "Log in with Google"

  • Login is optional. If you log in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app through Firebase Authentication.
  • Your game record (XP, coins, badges, games played, names of friends you played online) is saved in Firebase under your account, so it comes back on any phone when you log in.
  • The public "Everyone's rank" list shows only your app name, chosen face, level, XP and wins. Your email address and Google photo are never shown to other players.
  • Only you can read your saved record; database rules block everyone else.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your email to send messages.
  • There are no gambling or betting games.

5. Delete your data

  • In the app, open Main → Clear my record. This clears the record on your phone and, if you are logged in, in your cloud account.
  • To delete your account completely (including the rank list entry), email contact@nextgrade.in from the Gmail you logged in with. We delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The games are made for families. Children can play fully without login. If a parent wants a child's cloud record deleted, write to the email above.

7. Services used

Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) — Firebase privacy. Fonts from Google Fonts. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages.

8. Contact

contact@nextgrade.in · nextgrade.in
